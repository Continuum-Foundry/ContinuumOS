# Legal and rights policy

Effective project policy: 2026-10-18. Original project material is **all rights
reserved**, as recorded in [LICENSE](LICENSE). This is a proprietary research
repository, not a grant of open-source or commercial rights.

The owner's [U.S.-only handling instruction](EXPORT_CONTROL.md) imposes an immediate
remote-transfer hold. No new upload, push, disclosure, or remote processing is
approved. Geographic containment and legal export classification remain unverified.

## Scope and ownership

The reservation covers original source, scripts, schemas, documentation, and
artwork to the extent protectable rights exist. Upstream components, their license
texts, third-party quotations, and separately identified material keep their own
terms. [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) identifies those boundaries.
The original banner is vector artwork, not a screenshot or a shipped desktop.

Repository administration under `Continuum-Foundry` does not establish an
incorporated company, transfer copyright, or establish ownership of every file.
Each rights holder retains the rights they actually hold. Commit authorship,
access permissions, and GitHub handles are not substitutes for chain-of-title
records. Do not invent an LLC, legal address, registration number, or assignment.

GitHub access is limited by repository permissions and applicable platform terms.
This policy does not remove rights supplied by law, upstream licenses, existing
agreements, or [GitHub's terms](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service).
Repository visibility is a separate administrative setting. Keep it private
pending the publication review below.

## Changes, contributions, and permissions

External code, documentation, and artwork submissions are paused. A DCO sign-off
alone would not establish the commercial rights this project might need.
There is no CLA, automatic assignment, or implicit relicensing policy. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Rights grants, commercial permissions,
licensing changes, and assignments require a separate written agreement approved
by the relevant rights holders. An administrator's merge approval is not that
agreement. Do not retrospectively sign for another author.

For permission requests, open an issue asking the administrator for an appropriate
private channel; include no confidential business or personal information.
No support, service, sale, or confidentiality agreement is created by an issue.

## Publication and commercial readiness

The present U.S.-only/remote-transfer hold blocks publication and external
distribution. If a later review considers any permitted domestic delivery,
before a source release, binary distribution, customer agreement, or
ownership claim, the administrator must record a review of:

1. Actual rights holders and any employee, contractor, or prior contributor
   agreements, including permissions for generated or AI-assisted material.
2. Upstream licenses for the exact shipped files and selected license alternatives;
   complete notices, source-delivery obligations, and any commercial licenses.
3. Final product identity, trademark clearance, legal entity and authorized signers.
4. A reviewed contributor agreement and customer terms if either is needed.
5. Privacy, security reporting, export/sanctions, and distribution requirements
   relevant to the actual markets and delivered product.

These reviews are **not completed by adding policy files**. This repository does
not claim incorporation, registered marks, legal compliance certification,
patent clearance, security certification, or release clearance. Legal review by
qualified counsel remains a release gate. Technical release gates are in
[RELEASE.md](RELEASE.md).
