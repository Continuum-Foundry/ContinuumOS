# Code of conduct

Treat people with respect in project issues, reviews, discussions, and other
project-managed spaces. Be direct about technical disagreements and considerate
about the people involved. Focus criticism on claims, behavior, and evidence.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, deliberate
disclosure of confidential information, and impersonation are not acceptable.
Do not pressure people to disclose personal information or move conversations
into unsafe channels. Respect boundaries and a request to stop unwanted contact.

The administrator may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation, or
revoke repository access. Consider context and severity; serious threats or
disclosures may require immediate action. Communicate the reason when appropriate
without exposing the affected person's private information.

Report concerns to the administrator identified in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)
through an existing trusted private channel. If none exists, request a private
conduct contact without identifying the affected person or posting evidence.
Do not post private allegations or sensitive screenshots in issues. No dedicated
confidential reporting service or response-time commitment exists yet. Reports
should be shared only with people who need them for review; absolute secrecy
cannot be promised.

For concerns about an administrator, use another authorized organization owner
if available or the hosting provider's abuse-reporting process. This project
does not claim to have an independent appeals panel. Security defects use
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
